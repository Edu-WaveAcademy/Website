FAQ

Questions parents usually ask before joining.

How does the student portal work without sharing Google files directly?

Students sign in to Eduwave with portal credentials. The backend checks the assignment map and serves read-only views of the material instead of giving direct permanent Drive access.

How are fees handled during the trial phase?

Fees can still be paid manually through UPI or your existing method. The portal only tracks status, reference, and reminder flow until paid automation is needed.

Can younger children use the platform easily?

Yes. The student experience is designed around large buttons, clear labels, and a smaller number of actions rather than a complex dashboard.

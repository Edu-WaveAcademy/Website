Good to know / 06

Before you begin.

Simple answers for parents using the trial-phase portal.

Will I need access to Google Drive or Sheets?+

No. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your approved email, and the portal shows only the resources assigned to your child.

Can one parent view more than one child?+

Yes. Children linked to the same approved parent account appear as separate tabs inside the parent dashboard.

Can I pay through the website?+

During the free trial phase, the portal shows UPI instructions and lets you submit the payment reference for academy verification.

How will reminders work?+

Pending assignments, exams, and fee updates appear after login. The academy can also send a prepared WhatsApp reminder using the admin dashboard.
